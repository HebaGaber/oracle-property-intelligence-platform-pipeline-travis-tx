In short

Visitors need no account, and there are no ads. The only accounts are for the few admins who run the data pipeline. We keep a short log of server-side events, and nothing in it identifies you.

What we record

Each AI question, MCP call, Try-a-tool call and search, with its result count, response time and any failure reason. No user IDs, emails or IP addresses. Kept 30 days, then deleted. Filters and SQL run in your browser and are not logged.

What stays in your browser

Your starting area, saved searches and this session’s AI question count. Your location is read only when you tap “Use my location” and is not stored.

Admin accounts

Admins sign in with an email code (Amazon Cognito, AWS region eu-north-1). We store only the admin’s email address, for as long as they are an admin, and delete it within 30 days of a request or of their access ending. Admin emails never appear in the activity log.

Other services

Map tiles come from [MAP TILE PROVIDER] (Map view, CRM preview only). Street View photos are not used yet (coming soon). AI mode sends your question to the model provider set for this deployment (Amazon Bedrock or Vercel AI Gateway).
