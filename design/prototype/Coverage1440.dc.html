Known limitations

  • BBB: Blocked · bbb.org refused the check (403) on 2026-10-17. No ratings loaded in Run 3; ratings show, with their date, where a later run can collect them.
  • Permits: Austin’s data covers the City of Austin only. Pflugerville, Manor and unincorporated areas have no permit source yet.
  • Owners: some owner records are withheld as confidential and show as “withheld”, not blank.
  • Contractors: Austin permits carry names only, so most names stay Candidate or Unresolved. Resolved needs an official TX Comptroller taxpayer number.
  • Roof age: an estimate: reroof permit first, then new-build date, then year built, with a confidence badge.
