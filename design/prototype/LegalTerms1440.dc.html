What CountyLens is

A free tool for exploring public property, permit and contractor records for Travis County, TX. It is not legal, financial or construction advice.

Using the data

Each dataset keeps its own licence; see Data use and licences. Values can be wrong or out of date, so check the source chip before relying on one.

Fair use

Rate limits apply to search, the helper and the gateways. Do not use owner information to harass anyone, or in ways the source licences forbid.

The AI helper

Answers come only from published data, cite their records and state their assumptions. They can still be incomplete; open the cited records to check.

Availability

Counties marked “Coming soon” have no data. Features may change; published CIDs stay retrievable from public gateways.
