## Draft acceptance criteria (put these in the story file, then edit)

As a **data operator**, I want **one place to see runs, coverage, the manifest, verification and roofing queries**, so that **every UI line of the demo transcript works on the hosted URL**.

1. Given the Runs, Coverage, Manifest and Verify, Lead finder and SQL screens, when opened on the hosted URL, then each matches design/ and shows provenance.
2. Given the Lead finder, when a pin, a 5 mi radius and roof age 15 are set, then results show roof-age basis, coordinates and source.
3. Given open roofing permits, when listed, then they are sorted by open duration with contractor and BBB or not available.
4. Given owner tenure over 10 years or out-of-area owners, when filtered, then matching properties are returned.
5. Given a 390 px phone, when the app opens, then the mobile layout (full-bleed map, bottom sheet, bottom navigation) works.
6. Given any screen, when data is loading, empty, partial or failing, then the designed state shows.
