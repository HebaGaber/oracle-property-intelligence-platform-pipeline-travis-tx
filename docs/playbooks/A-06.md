## Draft acceptance criteria (put these in the story file, then edit)

As a **data operator**, I want **every available permit, with roofing permits classified and their open duration preserved**, so that **long-open roofing permits can be found and ranked**.

1. Given the Austin Socrata dataset, when the backfill runs, then full history is loaded and later runs are incremental by the :updated_at watermark.
2. Given each supported jurisdiction, when its vendor module runs, then its permits load; records-request and no-public-history ones are recorded with evidence.
3. Given a permit, when it is loaded, then status, issue, expiry and completion dates and the open duration are preserved.
4. Given the frozen roofing profile, when classification runs, then roofing permits are classified through the profile and needs_review terms are listed.
5. Given a permit that matches no property, when it is loaded, then it is kept as unmatched, not dropped.
