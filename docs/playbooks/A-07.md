## Draft acceptance criteria (put these in the story file, then edit)

As a **roofing sales rep**, I want **contractor names on permits linked to official business records**, so that **contractor details are trustworthy**.

1. Given the Comptroller Active Franchise Tax Permit Holders, when loaded, then it is the official business registry.
2. Given a permit contractor name and address, when matching runs, then a company edge is written only on an unambiguous official match, with the rule and its version.
3. Given matching finishes, when counts are reported, then resolved, unresolved and ambiguous counts are shown.
4. Given an ambiguous match, when it is shown, then the UI says contractor unresolved instead of guessing.
