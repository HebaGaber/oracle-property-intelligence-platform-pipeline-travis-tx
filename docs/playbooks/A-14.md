## Draft acceptance criteria (put these in the story file, then edit)

As a **roofing sales rep**, I want **to ask property questions in plain English and get source-backed answers**, so that **I find leads without writing SQL**.

1. Given 'Which properties in Travis County within five miles of Pflugerville have roofs older than 15 years?', when asked, then real matching properties return with reasoning and source evidence.
2. Given 'Which properties near that area have open roofing permits that have been open for many years, and who is the listed contractor?', when asked, then permit age, contractor and BBB (when available) return.
3. Given any answer, when shown, then assumptions (centre, radius, thresholds, many years = N) are stated as editable chips.
4. Given missing data, when it affects the answer, then a missing-data note says what and how much.
5. Given a question outside the data, when asked, then the agent says it cannot answer instead of guessing.
