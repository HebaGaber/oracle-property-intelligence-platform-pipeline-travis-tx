## Action

1. **Carrier check.** The PO loaded the lexicon references (A-1). mew and oracle consulted read-only (`specs/002-data-contract/agent-design.md`). The verdicts were re-checked against the live schema cache filled by the pilot's `validate`, and against `DESCRIBE` of the pilot tables.
2. **Pilot.** The pilot ran locally on the PO's D-3 slice: 42 Austin reroofs issued 2026-09, 5 Expired BP reroofs, 3 older Final BP reroofs, and their TCAD parcels. It used elephant-cli 1.58.1 at commit 44bb182, in the order validate → hash with CAR → validate CAR → export-tables, per data group. Nothing was uploaded and `--atlas-page` was not used.
3. **Views and contract.** The four views (`packages/query-core/views.sql`) were bound to the pilot export, and the 20 sample rows were cut from them.
4. **Profile.** The profile `travis-austin@1.0.0` was measured live against the full Austin dataset (AC-5). A 25-permit sample went to the PO for review (AC-3, D-7).
5. **Gazetteer.** `travis-places@1.0.0` was built from the public Census 2024 files.
6. **Independent test.** The story's three commands were run (below).
