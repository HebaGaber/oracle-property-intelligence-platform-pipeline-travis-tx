No official TCAD state-code table is on disk (`APPRAISAL_STATE_CODE.TXT` is not in the export), so nothing is guessed as SingleFamily or Condominium. **28,595 of 441,961 R accounts (6.5%) use the fallback**; top codes O1 13,043 · E1 6,514 · D1 2,902 · F4 2,891 · F5 1,286 · F3 847 · E2 453 · E3 356 · D2 201 · A5 21. A4 (54,675 accounts) is typed `Townhouse` (deprecated, condominium reading unverified, DEC-A-01); F4 (2,891) gets the generic fallback. Rule version `property_type_rules@2026-10-22` is stored per row. Published as source limitations `property_type_improvement_fallback` and `property_type_unverified_state_codes`.
