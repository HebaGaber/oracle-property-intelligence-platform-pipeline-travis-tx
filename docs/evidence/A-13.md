Hand measurement of the engine file on the preview: 1.1 to 1.5 MB/s (6.0, 7.2, 5.3 s) at 14:55, and 0.39 then 0.78 MB/s at 16:50. The SQL panel's waterfall on `2e14516`: engine `wasm.gz` 8.09 MB took 9.3 s, the `icu` extension 1.9 MB 3.1 s, `parquet` 0.74 MB 1.8 s, `json` 0.19 MB 0.9 s (about 11 MB in parallel), and everything else was done by about 4 s. The waterfall no longer includes Google (ERR-46). So on the link of that evening the 11 MB cannot arrive inside 8 s; on the faster link of the morning it did.

**Plainly: TC-040 has two measurements and passes in CI.** It failed on the author's slow home link on `2e14516` (11 to 26 s at 0.4 to 1.5 MB/s), it passed earlier on the same link when the link was faster (4.1 to 4.6 s), and it **passes on the GitHub runner in the final CI run 39835352646** (13 of 13 in the Verify, performance and vitals step). PO decision (2026-10-22): keep the 8 s limit and record both measurements, no code change. Detail of the slow-link failure: The fonts change (ERR-46) removed 3rd-party requests and cannot make the 8 MB download faster. PO decision (2026-10-22, "Record both measurements, keep the 8 s limit (Recommended)"): the limit stays 8 s, no code change; G7 lists TC-040 as "passes at 1.4 MB/s or faster" (arithmetic: the roughly 11 MB the page fetches in parallel in 8 s is about 1.4 MB/s; the engine file alone, 8.09 MB in 8 s, is about 1.0 MB/s). The next re-measure is on a link whose speed is recorded, and again at A-11 with the full county.
