## 1. Ownership: what exists, what is missing

Published in Run 2 (tables): `person` (first_name, last_name; 815 rows), `company` (name; 22,741 rows; `sunbiz_document_number` empty), `sales_history` (`ownership_transfer_date`; 266,317 rows), edges `sales_history_has_person` (815) and `sales_history_has_company` (22,741), `*_has_mailing_address` (815 and 22,736 edges, pointing at address ids). Sale prices are never written (Texas non-disclosure). The `address` table (465,512 rows) has only id columns, so no text: situs address, owner mailing city, state and ZIP are in the CARs (`unnormalized_address`, and for mailing addresses `city_name`, `state_code`, `postal_code`, `plus_four_postal_code`) but not in the Parquet tables, because `export-tables` skips the `address` oneOf properties (R7). Result: `city_name`, `postal_code`, `unnormalized_address`, `owner_mailing_postal_code`, `owner_mailing_state_code` are `not_in_export` for all 441,961 rows. The street line of an owner's mailing address is deliberately never written ("never a mailing street", `slimPayload`).

Contact fields: the TCAD export has none for owners. `owners[]` has no phone or email key (closed key set, full scan). The only phone in the export is `owners[].agents[].contactPhone`, the tax-protest agent's number (3,415 of 3,460 agent records in the sample), which is not the owner's and should not be published as owner contact. The Comptroller extract has no phone or email either (five fields only). The pinned lexicon `person` (10 properties) and `company` (4 properties) have no phone, email or URL property, so even the permit's `contractor_phone` (4,480 of 15,025 published roofing permits) has no carrier. Honest answer: no owner contact data exists in the sources we hold, and no contact field exists in the lexicon.

Owner-related raw TCAD fields we do NOT publish (from the closed key list; read but not written, or not read):

- `owners[]`: `addrDeliveryLine` (mailing street), `addrUnitDesignator`, `addrFreeForm`, `addrFreeForm1/2/3`, `carrierRoute`, `deliveryPoint`, `deliveryPointCheckDigit`, `cassValidationBy/Dt/Service`, `autoCass`, `latitude`, `longitude` (geocode of the mailing address, 1,949 of 5,355 in the sample), `nameSecondary` (836), `spouseFirstName`, `spouseLastName`, `ownerID`, `pAccountID`, `ownerPct`, `ownerTaxable`, `ownerValue`, `exemptions` (3,374 of 5,355 owners; sample shape includes `exemptionCode`, `beginDt`, `qualifyYr`: a homestead flag would mark owner-occupied), `applyPctExemptions`, `agents` (tax representatives), `referenceID`, `regTag`, `source`.
- `sales[]`: `buyerLine` (4,524 of 5,006 sales) and `sellerLine` (4,359): the buyer name of the latest sale could tie an owner to a sale, which the limitation text says the export lacks; `deedID`, `saleType` (published only as a transfer date), `sourceOfSale`, `realtor`; sale prices intentionally excluded.
- Top-level `deeds[]`: `deedDt`, `deedRecordedDt`, `deedType`, `instrumentNum`, `volume`, `page`, `buyerLine`, `sellerLine`, `consideration` (33,476 deeds in the 5,355-record sample, 6.2 per record). Also `events`, `links`, `tags`, `smartgroups` (not owner data).
- Owner name shapes: raw `name` is all capitals; only 25 of 5,355 sample owners carry `firstName` and `lastName`; 1,264 match the entity pattern; 4,091 match neither, so they are not written. Confidential accounts: 15,410 redacted in Run 2 (230 of 5,355 in the sample).

More owner data from sources already held: yes for names (the individual owners are in the roll we already captured), mailing city/state/ZIP/country for every non-confidential owner (in the roll, and already in the CARs for company owners), homestead exemption, deed dates. The Comptroller rows are companies with a `mailingAddress`; they could give the registered address of an owner LLC but need a name match, which is a candidate link and not identity.

## 3. BBB: what to change

What `v_roofing_permit` expects today: `bbb_status` (enum `rated`, `not_found`, `blocked`, `not_checked`), constant `not_checked` from `bbb_status()` in `derive.ts`; `bbb_status_reason` = `contractor_unresolved` or `no_lexicon_carrier`. The missing-reason vocabulary already has `bbb_blocked` and `bbb_not_found` (`packages/contract/src/provenance.ts`), but `derive.ts` never returns them.
